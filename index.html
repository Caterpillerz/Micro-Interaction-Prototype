Ready to calibrate your gaze?

Before you begin reading, let's help the reader learn where you're looking.

  1. Sit comfortably, keep your face visible in the camera, and try to keep your head in the same position.
  2. Eight dots will appear around the reading area in a shuffled order, followed by one in the center.
  3. Look directly at each dot and click it three times without looking away. It will turn green when complete.
  4. After all nine points, look at the center dot without clicking for a few seconds. This checks your gaze estimate.

Take your time. There's no countdown. Good lighting and clearly visible eyes help tracking.

Great job! Your calibration points are complete.

One quick check remains. A blue dot will appear in the center of the reading area.

  1. Look directly at the blue dot as soon as it appears.
  2. Keep your eyes on it for a few seconds.
  3. Don't click the dot or move your head around.

We'll use this quick check to estimate how closely your gaze predictions match the point you're looking at.
